About Ai-sle

Built inside a
real supermarket.

Ai-sle wasn't built by a software agency that studied the grocery industry. It was built by someone who owns two FreshChoice stores and got tired of doing the same manual tasks every week.

Every tool started as a solution to a real problem in a real store. Now we're making those solutions available to every independent supermarket in New Zealand.

From the founder
"I used to spend hours every week on roster formatting, variance checking, and payroll entry. I knew there had to be a better way — so I built it. Now those same tasks take minutes, and I want every FreshChoice owner to have that time back."
Saahil — Founder, Ai-sle
Owner, FreshChoice Titirangi & Hauraki

Where it started

It started with the roster. Every week, the same process — exporting from the scheduling system, manually formatting a PDF, sending it to 20+ staff one by one. An hour of work that should take five minutes. So we automated it. Then we did the same for variance checking, payroll, and KPIs. Four tools later, Ai-sle was born.

Where we're going

We're starting with FreshChoice because that's where we can be most useful — we know the systems, the reporting structure, and the pain points inside out. From there, we plan to expand across independent grocery in New Zealand, building tools that fit the way real stores actually operate.

Technology

Battle-tested tools.
Built for real stores.

Every tool in our stack has been through our own stores first. We don't experiment on your time — we adopt what works.

🤖
Claude by Anthropic
AI Engine
The AI model powering every Ai-sle tool. Claude is one of the most capable and reliable large language models available — it reads your files, understands context, and produces accurate, structured outputs every time. We chose Claude for its consistency and its ability to handle real-world document formats without hallucinating.
🐍
Python
Automation & Processing
The backbone of every automation pipeline. Python handles file reading, data transformation, document generation, and the logic that connects your uploads to the final outputs. It's the most battle-tested language for data processing — reliable, fast, and capable of handling any file format your store uses.
📄
PDF & Document Generation
Output Layer
Every tool produces a polished, ready-to-use output — whether that's a formatted PDF roster, a Word document for your variance check, or an Excel wage report. We use industry-standard libraries to generate documents that look professional and work straight away, with no manual formatting needed.
📧
Gmail Integration
Communications Layer
Tools like the Roster Builder connect directly to Gmail to send outputs to your staff automatically. No copy-pasting, no manual sending — the tool handles the whole workflow from file upload to staff inbox. Built on Google's API so it's reliable, secure, and uses your existing email address.
📁
Google Drive & Excel
File Handling
Your files stay in tools you already use. Uploads go via Google Drive or direct file input, and outputs land in Excel, Word, or PDF depending on the tool. No new software to learn, no new accounts to manage — everything works with the systems your store already runs on.
⚙️
Custom Pipelines
Automation Architecture
Each tool is a purpose-built automation pipeline — not a generic template adapted for grocery. The pipeline reads your specific file format, applies your store's rules and structure, runs it through the AI layer, and produces the output. Built once, configured for your store, runs reliably every week without touching it.

Security & data
Your data is yours.
Full stop.

We take data handling seriously. Here's exactly how we treat your store's information.

🔒
No data stored
Files you upload are processed and immediately discarded. We don't store your roster data, your payroll figures, or your variance reports. Each run is stateless — nothing persists after the output is generated.
🛡️
Encrypted in transit
All file uploads and outputs are transmitted over encrypted connections. Your data never travels over an unsecured channel, from upload to final output delivery.
👤
Your accounts, your control
Tools that connect to Gmail or Google Drive use your own credentials via Google's secure OAuth flow. We never see your password and access can be revoked by you at any time from your Google account settings.
🇳🇿
NZ-based operation
Ai-sle is a New Zealand business. We operate under NZ privacy law and the Privacy Act 2020. You know who you're dealing with — a store owner two calls away, not an anonymous overseas platform.
